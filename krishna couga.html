About Krishna Couga

"German opens doors — to Europe, to opportunity, to yourself."
— Krishna Couga

A dedicated German trainer based in Lawspet, Pondicherry — teaching online, reaching everywhere.

With 8+ years of experience, Krishna Couga has helped 500+ students across India master the German language — from A1 beginners to B1 level and beyond. Specialized in online teaching with a methodology that combines cultural immersion, structured grammar, and practical conversation.

All classes are conducted fully online via Google Meet / Zoom, making quality German education accessible to students across India. Special focus on Goethe Institute exam preparation.
